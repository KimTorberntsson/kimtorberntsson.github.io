The whole family visited Kolmården at Halloween this year. We mostly watched the small theatre acts, but there was also time for watching the animals and visiting Bamses Värld.

Most of all I was happy to finally get a good look at a snow leopard. We have been there several times by now, but this was the first time one showed itself properly. On the way in I also saw a [wolverine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolverine) (yeah, it really is called that) for the first time, as they are usually very elusive.

Hoping we'll have time for a visit this Christmas season as well.
